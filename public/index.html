linux desktop

Since May 2024 I've been daily driving Linux on all of my computers.

At first, I used NixOS to benefit from its declarative configuration system. From doing so I learned a lot about how Linux works and some concepts from functional programming. The learning process was pretty difficult, but I did have a lot of fun customizing hyprland.

Later, I felt too bothered by the effort needed to write configs and make things work in NixOS. So I decided to switch to Kubuntu in June 2025 and sticked to almost all default settings to minimize setup time. At this point, I thought that this approach to using linux was more time-efficient. Little did I know how wrong I was. T_T

In March 2026, I had the urge to try a new distro on my then newly acquired laptop. I went with Arch Linux because its wiki had left a good impression for me when I was using it to help write my nixos configs. It ended up being my favorite distro to date because it was even more simple to use than ubuntu if you have some prerequisite knowledge on how linux works. The instructions on the wiki are straight forward, the packages are up to date, it doesn't force users to use snap, and the system felt snappier and booted up faster for me.

I'll definitely keep using linux for most of my computing tasks, and only boot into windows for software only supported there.

forever Minecraft

The video game I spend the most time playing is modded Minecraft. I currently have a singleplayer "forever world" started back in Oct 2024, currently at around day 1700. My modlist is always changing, but the selection is always focused on food, building, and creating history inside the world.

"Forever worlds" are minecraft worlds that you commit to keep playing on, instead of creating new ones and abandoning it after a short time. Playing like this makes the world mean a lot more to players. For me, I also journal with book and quills sometimes. I ended up with a bookshelf full of snapshots of my life since I started the world. It's really enjoyable. :))

The main mods I use are:

Farmer's Delight, the Let's Do series, and other farming mods

These mods provide a lot of new crops and meals to cook. A lot of the meals are also placeable in the world, which is great for interiors. I do dislike Pam's harvestcraft, however, because most of its items only have one or two uses, unlike other farming mods where the number of items are low, but the combinations are numerous. I especially like mods that provide alternative ways of cooking and preparing food. (Fermentation my beloved)

Via Romana

A mod that allows you to fast travel after creating paths between locations. It incentivises the building of transportation networks, which is in line with what I like to do.

Exposure

This mod adds cameras and photos to the game, which makes recording the history of the world a lot more immersive and fun. I love taking pictures of random weird stuff I come across and pictures of newly constructed builds.

Touhou Little Maid

This mod provides lively NPCs that walk around and do work like players, making my builds feel more alive. It's also great for automating certain parts of the game without building industrial machinery. The companion aspect of the mod doesn't appeal to me that much though.

Create

I wanted a tech mod to automate the generation of building blocks. Out of the many options available, I chose the most popular option of Create because it fits better with the vanilla block palette. Whenever I want to focus on building, I take a bunch of building blocks from my vaults, put on a netherite jetpack and build in semi-creative mode.
